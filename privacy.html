PRIVACY

Your info stays your business.

Last updated: November 2026

When you submit our quote form, we collect the name, email, optional phone number, ZIP code, dog count, preferred service frequency, and yard details you choose to provide. We use this information to answer your request, provide a quote, and arrange service.

Form processing

Website quote forms are processed through the third-party FormSubmit service and delivered to our business email. Please do not submit gate codes, payment card details, health records, or other sensitive information in the form. FormSubmit may process information according to its own policies.

Photos and dog birthdays

If you become a customer, we may ask separately for your dogs' names, birthdays, dietary restrictions, and treat preferences to provide optional personalized service. We will not post your dog's photograph, birthday information, or a customer testimonial publicly without your separate permission. You may decline treats and social media participation.

Sharing and contact

We use your information for PooFecto! operations and the service providers necessary to run the website and respond to inquiries. We do not sell customer contact information. You can request corrections or deletion of customer information, subject to legitimate recordkeeping obligations, by contacting poofecto@gmail.com.

Submitting a quote request permits us to respond about that request, not to send unsolicited promotional texts. We may update this notice as services develop.
