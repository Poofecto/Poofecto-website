Privacy

Last updated November 2026

What we collect

When you request a quote, we collect your name, email, phone number (if you give it), ZIP code or neighborhood, number of dogs, how often you'd like service, and any notes about your yard.

If you become a customer, we'll separately collect your service address, how to access your yard, your dogs' names and details, treat and allergy preferences, and your permission choices for photos. We don't collect that on this website.

How the quote form works

Our quote form is processed by FormSubmit, a third-party service that forwards your message to our email inbox (poofecto@gmail.com). Your information passes through FormSubmit and is stored in our email account. Please review FormSubmit's own terms for how they handle data.

How we use it

We use your information only to answer your request, schedule and provide service, and bill you. We don't sell your information.

Photos of your dog

We only post photos or videos of your dog with your permission. Agreeing to treats is a separate choice from agreeing to photos, and you can take back permission at any time.

Your choices

To see, correct, or delete the information we have about you, or to stop texts or emails, email poofecto@gmail.com.
