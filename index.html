FAQ

Questions, answered

Do my dogs need to be inside during the visit?

Please keep dogs inside or secured during the visit, unless you tell us at sign-up that your dog is fine with us in the yard. If a dog is loose and it isn't safe to go in, we won't enter. We'll text you and wait a few minutes first.

How do you keep from spreading germs between yards?

Before every yard, we disinfect our scoop, rake and boot soles and put on fresh gloves. Dog waste can carry germs like parvo, giardia and roundworms, so every yard gets a clean start.

How does billing work?

Your price is per visit. Once a month, you're billed for that month's scheduled visits: 4 in most months, 5 in a few, depending on the calendar.

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as we can get into the yard safely, you don't need to be there. We'll text you a photo when we're done.

How many dogs can you handle?

Our standard pricing covers 1 to 4 dogs. Have 5 or more? Request a quote and we'll price it for you.

Do you do one-time cleanups?

Yes. One-time cleanups, twice-weekly service and first-time cleanups for overgrown yards are quoted once we know a little about your yard.

What's in the treat bag?

One store-bought dog biscuit per dog, in a small clear bag tied with lime-green ribbon. It's opt-in, and we ask about allergies first. We leave it in a spot you choose, out of your dogs' reach.

Will you post my dog online?

Only with your permission. Saying yes to treats isn't saying yes to photos, and you can change your mind at any time.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Bakersfield and building our routes now. Send a quote request with your ZIP code and we'll let you know if you're on our route.
