GOOD QUESTIONS

The scoop
on the scoop.

Do I have to be home during cleanup?

Usually not, as long as we have agreed on safe yard access and any dogs are securely separated from the work area. We'll confirm arrangements before your first visit.

How do monthly payments work?

Weekly service is quoted per visit and billed once per calendar month based on the visits scheduled that month. A month with four weekly visits costs less than one with five.

What if I have more than four dogs?

No problem—just request a custom quote for your pack.

Are the dog treats required?

Not at all. Complimentary treats are optional and only provided with your approval. Please tell us about allergies or special diets; we'll skip treats when they're not appropriate.

Can you post my dog's birthday photo?

Only with your separate permission. You always control whether your dog's photo or video is shared on our social media pages.
