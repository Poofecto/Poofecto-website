POOFECTO! — WEBSITE PACKAGE
===========================

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- index.html : complete responsive marketing website
- styles.css : branding / design
- script.js : interactive pricing and mobile menu
- privacy.html : introductory privacy statement
- assets/ : logo reference art, derived dog illustration, and favicon

PUBLISH WITH CLOUDFLARE PAGES (FREE PLAN)
1. Create / sign into a Cloudflare account.
2. Open Workers & Pages > Create > Pages > Direct Upload (menu names may vary).
3. Upload ALL contents of this website folder, ideally by dragging the folder or uploading poofecto-site.zip. Do NOT upload a zip containing another outer directory layer if Pages expects index.html at the root.
4. Cloudflare provides a *.pages.dev preview URL. Test the site first.
5. Add custom domain poofecto.com in Pages > Custom domains. Follow the exact DNS prompts. For an apex custom domain, Cloudflare may ask to move nameservers from GoDaddy to Cloudflare; this does NOT mean transferring or repurchasing the domain. Be mindful of existing DNS records if any.

IMPORTANT: ACTIVATE QUOTE FORM BEFORE SHARING WEBSITE
- This site's form posts to FormSubmit.co using poofecto@gmail.com.
- The first submission requires email confirmation / activation at poofecto@gmail.com before the form will deliver subsequent inquiries.
- Send a test quote, open the email received at poofecto@gmail.com, and ACTIVATE the form.
- Test a second submission and verify delivery. If the test fails, do NOT advertise the form as working; customers can use the visible email link while you troubleshoot.
- This third-party solution may require CAPTCHA steps. It is not a CRM or automated customer database.

PRICING
- Weekly: 1 dog $20/visit; 2 $25; 3 $28; 4 $32.
- 4 visits = 4 times visit price; 5 visits = 5 times visit price. Charged once per CALENDAR month.
- Twice-weekly, one-time, 5+ dog / commercial: custom quote until those prices are approved.

BEFORE LAUNCH
- Verify approved LLC status / legal entity name; ensure legal and privacy policy statements are accurate.
- Check business licensing, insurance, and dog waste disposal compliance.
- Confirm which neighborhoods/ZIP codes are eligible for service.
- Test on desktop and mobile. Check live quote form, emails, pricing, and links.
- Dog birthday details and treat allergies should be collected only from enrolled customers through a secure intake process; the public quote form deliberately does not ask for them.
- Instagram and other social links can be added after accounts are created.

VISUAL DESIGN
- Color: lime #BAF820, deep green #101C16.
- The original squatting dog illustration was sourced from the user's uploaded artwork and lightly adapted to the site.
- The logo is a web-font/typographic interpretation. Replace with the original official transparent logo file when available for exact visual fidelity.
- Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts when online; fallbacks work offline.
